Foundational Pronouns, Greetings, and Telling Time
Obololo! Welcome to Origonese French Lesson 1! This branch bridges classic French roots with the structural magic and simplicity of universal Origonese. 
1. The Universal Pronoun: Ori
In standard French, pronouns change depending on person, group, and formality (Je, Tu, Il, Elle, Nous, Vous, Ils, Elles). In Origonese French, every pronoun referring to the self, other self, or group self collapses into one universal word: Ori. 
French Concept	Standard Meaning	Origonese French
Je	I / Me	Ori
Tu	You (informal)	Ori
Il / Elle	He / She	Ori
Nous	We / Us	Ori
Vous	You (formal/plural)	Ori
Ils / Elles	They	Ori
Key Rule: Whether you are referring to yourself, someone else, or a group, the pronoun is always Ori. 
2. Greetings & Social Expressions
•	Obololo! (oh-boh-loh-loh)
o	Meaning: Hello! / Bonjour! 
o	Usage: Universal greeting across all Origonese dialects. 
•	Ojentey (oh-jen-tee)
o	Standard French Root: Enchanté(e)
o	Meaning: Nice to meet you. 
•	Ogogo? (oh-go-go)
o	Standard French Root: Ça va?
o	Meaning: How's it going? 
•	Ori ogo. (oh-ree oh-go)
o	Standard French Root: Je vais bien.
o	Meaning: I am doing well / good. 
3. Asking for Time: O-ticy-tacy-wo
The Origonese French word for time is o-ticy-tacy-wo (oh-teeky-tacky-woah), inspired by the French clock tick (tic-tac). 
Option 1: "Do you have the time?" (Most Common)
•	Origonese French: Ori o-as-lo o-ticy-tacy-wo?
•	Pronunciation: (Oh-ree oh-ahs-loh Oh-teeky-tacky-woah?)
•	Standard French Root: Tu as l'heure ?
•	Breakdown:
o	Ori = You (universal pronoun) 
o	o-as-lo = have (from French as) 
o	o-ticy-tacy-wo = time 
Option 2: "What time is it?"
•	Origonese French: O-kel-lo o-ticy-tacy-wo?
•	Pronunciation: (Oh-kel-loh Oh-teeky-tacky-woah?)
•	Standard French Root: Quelle heure est-il ?
•	Breakdown:
o	O-kel-lo = What / Which (from French Quelle) 
o	o-ticy-tacy-wo = time 
4. Sample Conversation
Person A: Obololo! Ori o-as-lo o-ticy-tacy-wo?
(Hello! Do you have the time?)
Person B: Obololo! O-ticy-tacy-wo is 2:00!
(Hello! The time is 2:00!)
Lesson Summary
1.	Ori replaces all French pronouns (Je, Tu, Il, Nous, Vous, Ils). 
2.	Greet others with Obololo!, say nice to meet you with Ojentey, ask how it's going with Ogogo?, and reply with Ori ogo.
3.	Speak about time using o-ticy-tacy-wo.
