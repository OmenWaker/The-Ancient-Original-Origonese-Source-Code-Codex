o	Concept: Bending or stretching time to fit your intent.
o	Example: If Ori was running late for work and only had fifteen minutes of time to get there, Ori could spend some of their saved time to drive the full thirty minutes it takes to get to work inside of fifteen minutes of objective time—ending up at work right on time!
•	O-blinky-wo Omageia (Teleportation Magic)
o	Concept: Instantaneous movement through space by blinking from one coordinate to another.
•	O-drifty-wo Omageia (Driving Magic)
o	Concept: Hyper-smooth navigation and spatial awareness while driving, allowing perfect movement through traffic or long distances.
