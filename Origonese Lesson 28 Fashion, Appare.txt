Origonese Lesson 28: Fashion, Apparel, and Creation

Welcome to Lesson 28! Today, we are stepping onto the runway of the spiritual hologram to explore the vibrant world of fashion, apparel, and design. Whether you are tailoring custom samurai armor for your Ori-mon, picking out the best high-vibe streetwear across The How, or stitching together sacred garments, this lesson gives you all the terms you need to look utterly drippied out in truth!

Part 1: Vocabulary & Phonetic Breakdown

1. Style, Time & General Terms
•	Fashion: Flashy-wo (flash-ee-woah)
•	Flash: O-flashy-lo (oh-flash-ee-loh)
•	Fashionably late: flashy-wowy o-laty-wo (flash-ee-wow-ee oh-lat-ee-woah)
•	Late: o-laty-wo (oh-lat-ee-woah)
•	Later: o-laty-yo (oh-lat-ee-yoh)
•	Lately: o-laty-yoyo (oh-lat-ee-yoh-yoh)
•	Maybe: May-bo (may-boh)
•	Be: Obo (oh-boh)

2. Core Wardrobe & Outerwear

•	Clothes: clothy-wow’s (kloth-ee-wows)
•	Shirt / T-shirt: shirty-wow (shert-ee-wow)
•	Pants: pantsy-wow (pants-ee-wow)
•	Jacket: jacksy-wow (jak-see-wow)
•	Coat: coaty-wow (koht-ee-wow)
•	Sweater: sweatsy (swet-see)
•	Hoodie: hoodsy (hood-see)
•	Vest: vesty (ves-tee)
•	Coveralls: covy-allsy (koh-vee-awl-see)
•	Tank top: tanky-toppy (tank-ee-top-ee)
•	Turtleneck: turtle-necky (ter-tl-nek-ee)
•	Dress (clothing item): Dressy-besty (dres-ee-bes-tee)

3. Footwear

•	Shoes: shoey’s (shoo-eez)
•	Sneakers: kicky’s (kik-eez)
•	Boots: bootsy’s (boot-seez)
•	Boot (single): bootsy (boot-see)
•	High heels: heighty’s (hight-eez)
•	Tap shoes: tappy-shoesy (tap-ee-shoo-see)
•	Steel toes (single): steely-toesy (steel-ee-toh-see)
•	Steel toes (plural): steely-toesy’s (steel-ee-toh-seez)

4. Accessories & Headwear

•	Hat: hatsy (hat-see)
•	Gloves: glovies (glov-eez)
•	Tie: necky-dressy (nek-ee-dres-ee)
•	Scarf: necky-accessy (nek-ee-ak-ses-ee)
•	Necklace: necky-showy (nek-ee-shoh-ee)
•	Earrings: Ear-ringy’s (eer-ring-eez)
•	Rings (plural): Ringy’s (ring-eez)
•	Ring (singular): Ringy (ring-ee)
•	Watches (for wrist): ticky-wristy (tik-ee-ris-tee)
•	Glasses (for eyes): Lensy’s, glassy’s, or sightsy’s (len-seez / glas-eez / sight-seez)
•	Sunglasses: shiny-wo-blockies (shine-ee-woah-blok-eez)

5. Hair Styling & Special Hoods

•	Ponytail: ologo-tail (oh-loh-goh-tayl)
•	Braid: Weavy-wo (weev-ee-woah)
•	Scrunchie: scrunchy-wo (skrunch-ee-woah)
•	Hood (car hood): O-cover-lo (oh-kuv-er-loh)
•	Hood (ghetto / neighborhood zone): Hoody (hood-ee)
•	Neighbourhood: o-nayby-hoody (oh-nay-bee-hood-ee)

6. Design & Creation Tools

•	Designer: designy-wo (de-sign-ee-woah)
•	Design: o-designy-lo (oh-de-sign-ee-loh)
•	Stitch: o-stitchy-lo (oh-stich-ee-loh)
•	Sew: o-sewy-lo (oh-soo-ee-loh)
•	Needle: o-needly-lo (oh-need-lee-loh)
•	Needy: o-needsy-wo (oh-need-see-woah)
•	Need: o-musty-wo (oh-mus-tee-woah)
•	Must: o-musty-lo (oh-mus-tee-loh)
•	Took: Tooksy (took-see)


Part 2: Example Sentences in Origonese

Put your new fashion vocabulary into action across the coordinate grid of The How:
•	On Designing & Stitching:
The master designy-wo used an o-needly-lo to o-stitchy-lo a custom shirty-wow and matching pantsy-wow for the incoming o-modely-lolo.
•	On Footwear & Armor Styling:
"Make sure you wear your steely-toesy's and your heavy jacksy-wow before heading out into the o-omega-o-blowy-wo weather!"
•	On Accessories & Eyewear:
Under the bright green sky of Noctua, Ori put on his shiny-wo-blockies, adjusted his necky-showy, and checked the time on his ticky-wristy.
•	On Social Gatherings & Timing:
May-bo we will see the crew later, but Ori arrived flashy-wowy o-laty-wo because he was busy styling his hair into a clean Weavy-wo.
