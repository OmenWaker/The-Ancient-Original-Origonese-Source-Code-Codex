Part 2: Understanding Family Vibration & Love Bonds
In Origonese, family names use the smooth -lo suffix (Omomolo, Opapalo, Obibilo, Osisylo, Ogrammylo, Ogrampylo) because they represent flowing, eternal soul connections. Romantic titles (Girly-wo, Boyo-wo) incorporate the -wo suffix to carry a light, sparkler-like romantic energy, while life partners (Wife-o, Huz-bo) use grounded, loving anchors!
•	Expressing Love to Family: Remember from Lesson 1, you can combine these with O-lovey-lo!
o	Example: "Ori O-lovey-lo Ori's Obibilo and Osisylo!" → (I love my brother and sister!)
•	Celebrating Your Partner:
o	Example: "Ori's Girly-wo is pure high frequency!" → (My girlfriend has amazing energy!)
