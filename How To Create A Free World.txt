How To Create A Free World
Written By Omen Waker
	To transition from a society based on scarcity, economics, capitalism, control, and currency to a society where there is no money and everything is free, these steps must happen first: 
1.	The Vision Check: Everyone must be asked if they would be willing to continue doing what they are already doing and plan to do, for free, so that everything could be free for themselves and every single person on Earth. We must find out if everyone is willing to contribute and volunteer to society for free so that everything can be free. If a large majority of the population says yes, then nothing can stop it from happening. 
2.	The Planning Years: After it is known that people would rather volunteer for free than work for pay, new systems need to be created by humanity to replace the old ones. When all the plans are made, a date is picked to implement the new changes and bring total freedom to the planet. 
3.	The Beginning of True Freedom: In the beginning, everyone would continue doing what they already are while everyone works on getting the free education and training needed to volunteer for the position they truly want to spend their required volunteer hours doing. Everything would change and improve.
