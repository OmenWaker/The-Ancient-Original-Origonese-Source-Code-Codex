Part 1: Lobby Setup & Audio Gear

•	O-lobby-lo (Oh-lob-ee-loh)
o	Meaning: Game Lobby / Party Room / Waiting Space
o	Definition: The digital staging area where squad members gather before dropping into a match.
o	Example: "Ori is waiting in the O-lobby-lo for the rest of the squad!"
•	O-headdy-set-lo (Oh-head-ee-set-loh)
o	Meaning: Headset / Gaming Mic / Voice Chat Link
o	Definition: The audio hardware used to transmit high-vibe callouts over discord or party chat.
o	Example: "Plug in your O-headdy-set-lo so ori can O-listeny-lo the team!"

Part 2: Battlefield Supplies & Revival

•	O-looty-lo (Oh-loot-ee-loh)
o	Meaning: Loot / Gear / Floor Drops / Deathbox Supplies
o	Definition: Weapons, attachments, and armor scattered across the map. 
o	Example: "Check that supply bin for some ogogo O-looty-lo!"
•	O-healy-pop-lo (Oh-heal-ee-pop-loh)
o	Meaning: Healing Item / Shield Battery / Medkit / Phoenix Kit
o	Definition: Consumables used to restore health or shields mid-fight.
o	Example: "Hold on! Ori needs to pop an O-healy-pop-lo behind this rock!"
•	O-revivy-lo (Oh-ree-vive-ee-loh)
o	Meaning: To Revive / Pick Up a Downed Squadmate
o	Definition: Restoring a fallen teammate before their banner expires.
o	Example: "Get into O-sheltery-wo while ori comes to O-revivy-lo you!"
Part 3: The Ultimate Triumph
