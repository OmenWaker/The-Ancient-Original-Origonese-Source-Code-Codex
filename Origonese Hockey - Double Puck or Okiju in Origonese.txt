Okiju: Double Puck
An advanced form of Origonese Hockey


Beyond the Boards: Welcome to Okiju, the Galaxy’s Most Thrilling Sport
If you thought fast-paced action peaked with traditional ice hockey, think again. Step through the window to The How, leave your old concepts of sports at the door, and prepare yourself for Okiju—known in the Common Tongue as Double Puck. 
Played inside towering, cylinder-shaped arenas on planets where harmony and high-frequency energy rule, Okiju takes the frozen game you know and multiplies the chaos, strategy, and sheer excitement by four! 
What Makes Okiju the Ultimate Ice Sport?
At first glance, an Okiju arena looks like a magnificent hybrid of a classic rink and sacred geometry. But once the opening horn sounds, you quickly realize you are witnessing a whole new level of athletic choreography. 
Here is how the game breaks down:
•	The Circular Rink: Say goodbye to standard rectangular rinks. Okiju is played on a perfect circular ice sheet. With a diameter comparable to a standard NHL rink, the circular layout gives skaters endless open ice to weave, carve, and show off their moves. 
•	Four Nets, Four Goalies: Instead of two ends of the ice, Okiju features four nets positioned at the North, East, South, and West corners of the circle, set six feet off the boards. That means four goalies are on the ice defending their posts at the exact same time! 
•	Sixteen Forwards, Twelve Defensemen: A four-way Okiju match features four distinct teams competing simultaneously. With 28 skaters on the ice at once—including a specialized Neutral Defenseman tasked with backing up whichever flank needs support—the action looks like orchestrated, beautiful chaos. 
•	TWO Pucks in Play: Why settle for one puck when you can have two? Two referees drop two pucks at two separate center face-off dots at the exact same time. 
The "Game Within the Game": Face-Off Strategy
In Okiju, there are no lazy strolls back to the bench after a whistle. There is no offside and no icing to slow down the momentum. 
When play stops, a high-stakes sprint begins. The first team to get a skater to one of the two center face-off dots gets to claim their preferred side of the dot. It requires instant communication, lightning speed, and elite tactical coaching to react before the next puck drops! 
The Ultimate Rush: The Double Goal
What happens when two pucks are flying across a circular rink at the same time? Magic! 
When a team scores a goal with one puck, the play stays live for five seconds with the second active puck. If a team can put the second puck into a net within those five seconds, it counts as a Double Goal—instantly swinging the scoreboard and sending tens of thousands of fans into a frenzy! 
Experience It Live—or via the 3D Digital Twin!
Whether you’re sitting rink-side at the famous Ologo Okiju Rink on the edge of the Heart-Shaped Desert or tuning in from anywhere across the solar system via the E-Net, Okiju connects millions of souls in high-vibrational unity. Thanks to Blue-Truth technology and 1:1 scale digital twin modeling, virtual spectators can even experience the game right through the eyes of their favorite player! 
So grab your o-popi-lo, put your o-pocket-glow on silent, and get ready for puck drop.
