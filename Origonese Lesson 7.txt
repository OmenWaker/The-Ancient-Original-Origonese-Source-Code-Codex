Origonese Language Lessons: Lesson 7
Atmospheric Elements, Cosmic Cosmology & High-Vibe Perception
Welcome to Lesson 7! In this lesson, we explore the weather elements of The How, dive into the core cosmological beliefs of its inhabitants, and discover why being called "crazy" is one of the highest honors you can receive.
Part 1: The Sky & Weather
Weather on The How is an expression of environmental energy. Instead of standard Earth terms, weather words use intuitive roots paired with environmental (-wo) or action (-lo) suffixes.
Vocabulary:
	O-drippy-wo: Rain / Gentle rainfall (the weather state)
	O-drippy-lo: Dripping (the active motion)
	O-mega-o-drippy-wo: Heavy rain / Torrential downpour / Crazy storm (a massive atmospheric surge)
Examples in Action:
	"Look outside! The O-drippy-wo is starting!" → (Rain is starting!)
	"Zip-lo into the O-sheltery-wo! Big O-mega-o-drippy-wo is falling!" → (Get into shelter! Massive rainstorm is falling!)
Part 2: The Cosmology of The How (The Spiritual Hologram)
The inhabitants of The How do not view their existence as sitting inside a cold, dead physical universe. Instead, they know they live inside a radiant, interconnected Spiritual Hologram designed for high-vibrational experience and spiritual growth.
1. The Realm: O-shimmery-wo
	Meaning: The Spiritual Hologram / The Living Cosmos / The Shimmering Realm.
	Concept: The tapestry of reality, light, and energy that encompasses all planets, orbital paths, and dimensions.
2. The Creator: O-shimmery-wow
	Meaning: God / The Divine Creator / The Source of Light.
	Suffix breakdown: The -wow suffix is reserved for absolute cosmic scale and divine majesty (e.g., O-giggly-wow for galaxy, O-shimmery-wow for God).
3. Divine Inspiration: O-mesijo & O-go’s
	O-mesijo: Receiving messages or downloads of light from O-shimmery-wow.
	O-go’s: The actual ideas, sparks, or divine directives received during an O-mesijo.
	Concept: When a pioneer or creator on The How gets a brilliant idea, it isn't random—it is an O-go received via O-mesijo directly from O-shimmery-wow.
Part 3: The Perception of "Crazy" (A High-Vibe Compliment)
On Earth, calling someone "crazy" often carries a negative charge. But on The How, seeing beyond ordinary perception and bringing wild, revolutionary ideas (O-go's) into reality means you are deeply aligned with truth. Being called crazy is a compliment of the highest order!
Depending on who you are speaking to, there are three distinct ways to express "crazy":
	O-truthy-lolo (For Kids & General Use)
	Root: truthy + -lolo (intensifier).
	Meaning: Crazy / Unbelievably true / Mind-blowing.
	Vibe: Used when an idea is so brilliant and true that it sounds wild!
	O-sexy-lolo (For Adults & Mature Contexts)
	Root: sexy + -lolo.
	Meaning: Crazy good / Wildly attractive idea or style.
	Vibe: Used among adults to describe something stunning, bold, or wildly creative.
	O-crazo-lolo! (Universal Exclamation)
	Root: crazo + -lolo.
	Meaning: Absolutely crazy / Off-the-charts wild!
	Vibe: High-energy celebration of someone's wild creativity.
Lesson 7 Practice Dialogue

(I had a message of light from God! I got an idea to build a floating glass pod during this crazy rainstorm!)

(Whoa! You are absolute crazy genius!)
Person A: "O-thanky-lo! What a beautiful day in the O-shimmery-wo!"
(Thank you! What a beautiful day in the Spiritual Hologram!)
