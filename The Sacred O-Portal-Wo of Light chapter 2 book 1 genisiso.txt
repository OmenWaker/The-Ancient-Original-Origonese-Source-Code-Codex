The Sacred O-Portal-Wo of Light
Book 1: Genisiso

Chapter 2: Light, Fruit, and the First Shadow


	When the O-Shimmery-Wow made the heavens and the o-wiggly-wo's (planets), the o-wiggly-wow's (solar systems), and the o-giggly-wow's (galaxies), neither wild o-leaf-lo nor seeds were growing on any of the o-wiggly-wo's. For the O-Shimmery-Wow had not yet sent the o-drippy-wo (rain) to o-splashy-wo the o-wiggly-wo's, and there were no ori's to cultivate the soil. Instead, gentle o-splashy-pathy's rose up from the ground to o-splashy-wo all the land.
Then the O-Shimmery-Wow created the first sentient beings on many different o-wiggly-wo's across countless o-giggly-wow's at the exact same o-ticky-wo, shaping them from the dust of the ground. The O-Shimmery-Wow breathed the breath of life into their nostrils, and the diverse sentient beings became living, breathing souls.
History Note-A1: The Genesis Accord
Before any of this began—and before the very first sun-cycle when the O-Shimmery-Wow created light—Truth had already existed as the O-Shimmery-Wow. The O-Shimmery-Wow was never alone; He was simply the first point of consciousness to be birthed out of the great void. Soon, countless other points of consciousness followed behind, filling the higher spirit realms above any physical matter made of slowed-down light. Together, they created a perfect utopian paradise where ideas were shared freely.
However, a point of consciousness who named himself Evilon grew deeply jealous of the O-Shimmery-Wow and the fact that He was the first to exist. Evilon began working to undermine the O-Shimmery-Wow, recruiting followers to his cause until the O-Shimmery-Wow revealed His greatest creation yet: The Spiritual Hologram.
This was an infinite expanse where light was slowed down enough to interact with on entirely new levels—a realm of raw physicality. Every point of consciousness began debating and brainstorming how this physical hologram should be used.
Consumed by jealousy, Evilon became entirely corrupted. He vowed to ensure the O-Shimmery-Wow would lose control over His creation, plotting to turn the Spiritual Hologram into the exact opposite of what the creator intended.
Public debates ensued between the O-Shimmery-Wow and Evilon. The creator sought to prove that nothing is more powerful than light and truth, while Evilon argued that his concept of "Darkness" reigned supreme.
And so, before the O-Shimmery-Wow sparked light on the first sun-cycle-wo, an eternal understanding was struck: Free will would govern all forms of energy within the Spiritual Hologram. Neither creator nor shadow would directly interfere or provide blatant proof of their existence unless it was genuinely earned. The Spiritual Hologram was set to span hundreds of trillions of years.
The victor of this cosmic game would be declared supreme, granted any wish they desired. Knowing he could never win on merit alone, Evilon embedded a dark clause into the rules: whichever side influenced a sentient being more throughout their time in the Spiritual Hologram would claim rights to that soul.
While the O-Shimmery-Wow offered eternal freedom, love, and truth to all who chose the light, Evilon schemed to snare souls into darkness to overthrow the creator. Blinded by his own bitterness, Evilon never realized that his jealousy originated entirely from within—sparking the great cosmic struggle, and the birth of human emotion itself.

	After The O-Shimmery-Wow gave life to all the sentient beings the spiritual hologram had truly just begun. Different types of o-lifey-wow’s (trees) began growing all around them. After enough time fruit began growing just as they began to feel hunger for the first time and they tried eating the o-leaf-lo. Then a dark fruit that was black with white stripes began to grow from a dark ominous looking o-lifey-wow. It scared most of the beings except for a few who were drawn to the o-lifey-wow and ate the black fruit.
	These beings who ate the dark fruit were the first to turn from the light. Because they chose the obviously dark looking fruit Evilon was allowed to send them thoughts of darkness and block the light from reaching them. They became twisted and corrupt and left the other beings behind to start a better world away from all the happy people.
