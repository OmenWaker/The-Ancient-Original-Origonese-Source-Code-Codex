Overview
Omageia (Oh-mah-gee-ah) is a part of everyday life for every single person living in The Garden of Eden (what we call on Earth the Milky Way). Omageia is Origonese for "spirit magic." In truth, all magic is only possible through the power and wonder of the spirit, as well as through the blessings, gifts, ideas, and experiences that come from the Creator / Light / Truth.
When someone begins studying and practicing Omageia, they are called an Omago (oh-mah-go) if female, or an Omagi (oh-mah-gee, with gee sounding like "geese") if male. On The Heart of the World (or The How), there have only been a handful of dark practitioners, properly called Domago (female) and Domagi (male). The difference is that an Omagi knows that magic is a gift, while a Domagi believes it is something to be controlled and used for evil.
You will not find any dark Omageia in this text. Continue reading to discover what a beginner Omagi or Omago can learn and experience in the real o-wiggly-wo (world).
Chapter 1: Beginners' Omageia
Omageia is a mystical force originating from the Creator that allows magic to happen. It can be used to propel you forward or to teach valuable lessons. The first form of Omageia we will discuss is o-ticky-wo Omageia (time magic).
What is Time Magic?
Time magic can accomplish many things. Omageia can cause a person to step outside of time so that time stands still for you while moving normally for the rest of the world. This could allow someone to watch all three Lord of the Rings movies in just one hour before heading to work, or help you arrive on time when you were running late.
The key to using o-ticky-wo Omageia is switching your perspective from one filled with worry, doubt, and fear to one of humor, joy, and enthusiasm, maintaining a positive outlook. Omageia cannot function when you are in a state of worry, confusion, fatigue, or negativity. By shedding those o-negativy-wo shackles, Omageia fills the void. Because nature abhors a vacuum, positive energy (o-positivy-wo) naturally replaces the negative.
