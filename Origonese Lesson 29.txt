
Origonese Lesson 29: Entertainment, Recreation, and Movement

Welcome to Lesson 29! Today, we are diving straight into the vibrant entertainment, recreational activities, and dynamic movements of the spiritual hologram. Whether you are racing go-carts, gaming across your pocket-glow, or engaging in high-vibe sword play across The How, this lesson covers all the terms you need to keep your free time perfectly sparkly and action-packed!

Part 1: Vocabulary & Phonetic Breakdown

1. Media & Entertainment
•	Movies or films: o-shimmy-glows (oh-shim-ee-gloh-z)
•	Songs: o-memo-wow’s (oh-mem-oh-wowz)
•	Song: o-memo-wow (oh-mem-oh-wow)
•	Music: o-muziko (oh-moo-zee-koh)
•	Television shows: o-shimmy-wow’s (oh-shim-ee-wowz)
•	Video games: o-shimmy-gimmy’s (oh-shim-ee-gim-eez)
•	Games: gimmy’s (gim-eez)
•	Board games: boardy-gimmy’s (bohr-dee-gim-eez)
•	Computer games: computy-gimmy’s (kom-pyoo-tee-gim-eez)
•	Smartphone games / pocket-glow games: glowy-gimmy’s (gloh-ee-gim-eez)

2. Social Life & Fun
•	Bowling: o-rolly-smashy (oh-rol-ee-smah-shee)
•	Date: datesy (dayt-see)
•	Dating: datesy-wo (dayt-see-woah)
•	Fun: o-funsy-wo (oh-fun-see-woah)
•	Play: o-way (oh-way)
•	Go play: ogo o-way (oh-goh oh-way)
•	Away: ogoway (oh-goh-way)
•	Go away: ogo ogoway (oh-goh oh-goh-way)
•	Far: o-fary-wo (oh-fahr-ee-woah)
•	Far away: o-fary-wo ogoway (oh-fahr-ee-woah oh-goh-way)

3. Rest & Mindset States
•	Sleep: o-sleepy-wo (oh-sleep-ee-woah)
•	Meditate: elevaty-wo (el-ev-at-ee-woah)
•	Rest: o-resty-wo (oh-rest-ee-woah)
•	Laugh: o-laughy-wo (oh-lahf-ee-woah)

4. Movement & Action Verbs
•	Dance: dancy-wo (dan-see-woah)
•	Run: ogo-wo (oh-goh-woah)
•	Walk: ogo-lo (oh-goh-loh)
•	Skip: o-skippy-wo (oh-skip-ee-woah)
•	Swim: o-splashy-ogo (oh-splash-ee-oh-goh)
•	Swimming: o-splashy-ogo-yo (oh-splash-ee-oh-goh-yoh)
•	Float: o-floaty-lo (oh-floht-ee-loh)
•	Boat: o-floaty-wo (oh-floht-ee-woah)
•	Boating: o-floaty-yo (oh-floht-ee-yoh)
•	Sailing: o-splashy-drifty (oh-splash-ee-drif-tee)
•	River tubing: o-splashy-pathy-taxi (oh-splash-ee-path-ee-tak-see)

5. Racing & Competition
•	Go-carting: ogo-drifty-wo (oh-goh-drif-tee-woah)
•	Race: o-racey-wo (oh-ray-see-woah)
•	Racing: o-racey-yo (oh-ray-see-yoh)
•	Competition: o-testy-besty-wo (oh-tes-tee-bes-tee-woah)
•	Compete: o-testy-besty (oh-tes-tee-bes-tee)
•	Competing: o-testy-besty-yo (oh-tes-tee-bes-tee-yoh)
•	Competitor: o-testo-besto (oh-tes-toh-bes-toh)

6. Combat Arts & Training
•	Spar: o-sparo-wo (oh-spahr-oh-woah)
•	Fencing: ori-way-wo (oh-ree-way-woah)
•	Sword play: ori-way (oh-ree-way)
