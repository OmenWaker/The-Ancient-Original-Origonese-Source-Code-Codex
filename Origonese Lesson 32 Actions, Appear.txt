3. Aesthetics, Beauty & Descriptions
•	Beautiful: Obejutiful (oh-bej-oo-ti-ful)
•	Pretty: Pretty (5% Sacred Pillar Word)
•	Handsome: Josomo (joh-soh-moh) (jono = hand, somo = some)
•	Attractive: attract-wo (a-trakt-woah)
•	Ugly: Ogly (og-lee)
•	Cute: Cutesy (kyoot-see)
•	Gorgeous: Jiji-ogos (jee-jee-oh-gos)
•	Radiant: Oriant (or-ee-ant)
•	Odd: Oddomo (od-oh-moh)
•	Oddly: Oddomo-yo (od-oh-moh-yoh)
•	Strange: stroddo or o-strangey-wo (strod-oh / oh-strayn-jee-woah)
•	Stranger: stroddo-wo or o-strangey-wow (strod-oh-woah / oh-strayn-jee-wow)
•	Danger: Danger (5% Sacred Pillar Word) — Note: Danger is real; fear is not.
