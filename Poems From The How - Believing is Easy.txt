Believing is Easy

I remember being lost and confused, 
Playing only on my own team.
 Now I have finally found myself,
 Able to accomplish my dream.
Who is there to thank if not the Creator?
 My choices are aligned with the light,
 Receiving every idea I need to shine bright.
 We never go left—we always choose right.
We don’t invent; we discover
. Eternal truth is uncovered.
 Everything is a gift from God—
 Yet you still don’t believe?
What more do you need to receive?
 Have you not always been given everything you needed,
Every single time you needed it?
