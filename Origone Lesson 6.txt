Lesson 6: Online Video Gaming & Tactical Communication
Goal: Master high-energy team communication, spatial calls, and squad tactics for online gaming and action scenarios using Origonese!
1. New Vocabulary
Origonese Word	Root / Meaning	Context / Usage
O-preparo-lo	Ready / Prepared	Status check or state of readiness
O-fretty-lo	Worry / Fret	Stress or hesitation
O-sheltery-wo	Cover / Safe space / Shelter	Physical zone of protection
O-listeny-lo	Hear / Listen	Audio connection / Auditory check
O-sparky-wo	Spark / Vitality / Energy	The element or source of spark
O-sparky-lolo	"Let's do this!" / Ignite the action!	High-vibrational rallying cry
O-whippy-on
Or 
O-weapony-wo	Weapon / Gear	Tool for action
O-backy-lo	Behind us / In the back	Relative spatial orientation
O-backy-wo	Back there / Backwards / Back-up space	Physical area or direction behind
O-flanky-lo	Flank / Side maneuver	Tactical positioning
2. Grammar & Phrasing Breakdown
Calling Out Readiness & Clearing Worry
	Question: "Is Ori O-preparo-lo?" → (Are you / is everyone ready?)
	Answer: "Ori is O-preparo-lo, don't O-fretty-lo!" → (I am / we are ready, don't worry!)
Audio Check
	"Can any Ori O-listeny-lo Ori?" → (Can anyone hear me?)
Rallying the Squad
	"O-sparky-lolo!"
	Breakdown: Root spark + intensifier/action lolo.
	Vibe: "Time to make it happen!" / "Let's ignite the action!" / "Let's do this!"
3. Tactical Gaming Dialogue (In Action)
Practice reading this squad play call out loud:
Player 1: "Can any Ori O-listeny-lo Ori?"
Player 2: "Zip! Is Ori O-preparo-lo?"
Player 1: "Ori is O-preparo-lo, don't O-fretty-lo! Grab your O-whippy-on and O-sparky-lolo!"
Player 2: "Watch out—enemy O-backy-lo Ori!"
Player 1: "O-flanky-lo! Get to O-sheltery-wo, Ori!"
