Part 2: Traditional Academics vs. Classrooms on The How
On Earth, students study standard subjects like Onglisho (English), Mathy-wo (Math), Ori's-Story (History), Socially-Study-wo (Social Studies), and O-sentience-wo (Science). While these build great foundational knowledge, schools on The How also feature two core cosmic classes:
1.	Observo-wo (Observationism):
Students learn how to observe their environment without judgment, learning to perceive the hidden energy fields, light patterns, and spiritual harmony in everyday life.
2.	Imagey-wo Createy-wo (Imagination Creation):
Students are taught that imagination is a real building tool! In this class, students practice visualizing pure, positive ideas (O-go's) and bringing them into physical form through creative focus.
3.	Freebo Time! & Funny-wo ticky-wo:
P.E. (Funny-wo ticky-wo) and Recess (Freebo Time!) are considered essential for keeping your energy body balanced and high-vibrational!
