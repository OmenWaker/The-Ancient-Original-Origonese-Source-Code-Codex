•	Chat: o-chatty-wo (oh-chat-ee-woah)
•	Yell: uppity-wo (up-i-tee-woah)
•	Scream: panicky-wow (pan-ik-ee-wow)

•	Sing: angely-wo (an-jel-ee-woah)
•	Singer: angely-wow (an-jel-ee-wow)

•	Rhyme: rhyme (rym) — [5% Sacred Pillar Word]
