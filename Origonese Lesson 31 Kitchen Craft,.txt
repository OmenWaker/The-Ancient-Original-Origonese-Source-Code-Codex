Part 1: Vocabulary & Phonetic Breakdown

1. Kitchen & Culinary Utensils
•	Spoon: O-scoopy-lo (oh-skoo-pee-loh) — The tool to lift the world.
•	Fork: O-pokey-lo (oh-poh-kee-loh) — Any type of fork.
•	Butter knife: O-cutty-lo (oh-kut-ee-loh) — A dull blade for spreading spreads.
•	Sharp knife: O-knifey-lo (oh-nife-ee-loh) — Used for cutting O-leaf-lo.
•	Whisk: O-whisky-lo (oh-wis-kee-loh) — For whipping up high-vibe blends.
•	Spatula (tool): O-slappy-lo (oh-slap-ee-loh)
•	Slap (action): O-slappy-wo (oh-slap-ee-woah)
•	Utensils: U-gentles (yoo-jen-tuls)
•	Blender: O-blendy-lo (oh-blen-dee-loh)
•	Cooker (device): O-cooky-wow (oh-kook-ee-wow)
•	Cook (person): O-cooky-lo (oh-kook-ee-loh)
•	Fine dining cooking (art): O-cooky-wo (oh-kook-ee-woah)

2. Dining, Drinks & Vices
•	Eat: O-munchy-lo (oh-mun-chee-loh)
•	Snack (to snack): O-snacky-lo (oh-snak-ee-loh)
•	Snack (the item): O-snacky-wow (oh-snak-ee-wow)
•	Drink (the beverage): O-drinky-lo (oh-drink-ee-loh)
•	Drink (the act of drinking): O-drinky-wo (oh-drink-ee-woah)
•	Drinking: O-drinky-yo (oh-drink-ee-yoh)
•	Drunk: O-drunky-wow (oh-drunk-ee-wow)
•	Alcohol: Okohaul (oh-ko-hawl) (All-go-fall in the common tongue)
•	Juice: O-juicy-lo (oh-joo-see-loh)
•	Pop / Soda: O-popi-lo (oh-pop-ee-loh)
•	To pop something: O-popi-wo (oh-pop-ee-woah)
•	Popped: O-poppy-wow (oh-pop-ee-wow)
•	Popping: O-poppy-yo (oh-pop-ee-yoh)
•	Cup: O-splashy-holdy (oh-splash-ee-hole-dee)
•	Glass (drinking glass): O-glassy-lo (oh-glas-ee-loh)
•	Glass (actual material): O-glassy-wo (oh-glas-ee-woah)
•	Vape: O-vapey-glow (oh-vayp-ee-gloh)
•	Vapour: O-glowies (oh-gloh-eez)
•	Cigarette: O-puffy-lo (oh-puf-ee-loh)
•	Smoke (cigarette smoke): O-smokey-lo (oh-smoh-kee-loh)
•	Cigarette smoke: O-misty-lo (oh-mis-tee-loh)
•	Mist: O-misty-wo (oh-mis-tee-woah)
•	Lighter: O-lighty-wo (oh-lyte-ee-woah)
•	Flame from lighter: O-lighty-wow (oh-lyte-ee-wow)
•	Pack of smokes: O-packy-wow (oh-pak-ee-wow)
•	Pack: O-packy-wo (oh-pak-ee-woah)
•	Vape juice: Glowie-juicy-wo (gloh-ee-joo-see-woah)

3. Sky, Cosmos & Planetary Eruptions
•	Sunset: O-shiny-sleepy-wow (oh-shy-nee-sleep-ee-wow)
•	Sunrise: O-shiny-waky-wow (oh-shy-nee-wake-ee-wow)
•	Eclipse: Oclipso (oh-klip-soh)
•	Tornado: Tornajio (tor-nah-jee-oh) (Torny-window in common tongue)
•	Hurricane: Hurriko (hur-ee-koh) (Hurry-gain in common tongue)
•	Tsunami: Tsunajio (tsoo-nah-jee-oh) (Wave-killer in common tongue)
•	Earthquake: Groundy-shaky-wow (grownd-ee-shay-kee-wow) (Gaia-quake in common tongue)
•	Eruption: Oroshun (oh-roh-shun)
•	Erupted: Oroshuny (oh-roh-shun-ee)
•	Erupting: Oroshunyo (oh-roh-shun-yoh)
•	Erupt: Oroshu (oh-roh-shoo)
