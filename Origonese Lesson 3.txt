New Words for Lesson 3:
1. Origonese word for drink or to drink:
O-drinky-lo (Oh-drink-ee-loh) —or— O-drinky-lolo for a super delicious, sparkling drink!
•	Origonese Definition: Drink, to drink, or beverage.
•	Used in a sentence: Ori wants to o-drinky-lo some cold water after we o-munchy-lo!
2. Origonese word for water:
O-splashy-lo (Oh-splash-ee-loh)
•	Origonese Definition: Pure water, fresh water, or clean drinking water.
•	Used in a sentence: This o-splashy-lo tastes so clean and fresh!
3. Origonese word for drive or to move forward:
O-drifty-lo (Oh-drift-ee-loh)
•	Origonese Definition: Drive, to drive, hover, or drift smoothly.
•	Used in a sentence: Let’s ogogo o-drifty-lo around the o-wiggly-wo!
4. Origonese word for yes / affirmative:
Zip! (Zip!)
•	Origonese Definition: Yes! Absolute confirmation spoken with high joy.
•	Used in a sentence:
o	Question: Does ori love learning Origonese?
o	Response: Zip! Ori o-lovey-lo Origonese!
