Deep Lore: The Harmonic Calendar & Wishmas Day
To understand o-santy-gifty-wo omageia, you must understand how time is measured in high-vibrational alignment.
On Earth, calendars are fragmented, but the true cosmic standard is the Perfect Harmonic Calendar:
	13 Months per year
	28 Days per month
	364 Days total (13×28=364)
Because every month has exactly 28 days (4 perfect 7-day weeks), every month begins and ends on the exact same day. However, a full solar cycle requires 365 days.
Day 365: Wishmas Day
