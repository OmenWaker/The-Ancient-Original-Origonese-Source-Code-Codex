Part 3: Origonese Math Vocabulary & Equations
In Origonese, math isn't cold calculation—it's energy balancing! Note that because there is no concept of being "odd" or strange in a negative way, the word for addition borrows from high-vibe brightness.
•	Addition: Oddishy 
•	Plus / Add: Oddy 
•	Subtraction: O-takey-lo 
•	Subtract: O-takey-lo (Action: O-takey-wo) 
•	Multiply: O-mega-growy 
•	Divide: Eveny-lo 
•	Equals: Resulty's 
•	Equal / Results: Resulty / O-resulty-wo 
Example Equation:
O-apaty-lo oddy O-apaty-lo resulty's o-walo-lo
(4 + 4 = 8)
