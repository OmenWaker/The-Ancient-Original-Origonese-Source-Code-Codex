Deep Lore: Spiroguards, Entry Days & Temporal Mechanics
1. The Sacred Covenant of the Spiroguard
Every living, sentient being is assigned a Spiroguard on their Entry-wo Sun-cycle-wo (Entry Day / Birthday). Your Spiroguard is a dedicated guardian entity who tracks your frequency and protects your vessel from shadow attacks.
Whether an ori ever meets their Spiroguard face-to-face depends entirely on daily choices:
•	Low Vibrational Living: Heavy choices, anger, or o-murd-lo density obscure your spiritual vision. Your Spiroguard remains active in the background, but you will never hear or see them.
•	High Vibrational Living: Pure intention, love, and practicing omageia raise your frequency. Your Spiroguard becomes visible, walking alongside you as a best friend, advisor, and tactical ally in battle.
2. Time Travel Physics (O-Ticky-Travy-Wo Omageia)
Time is not a straight line; it is a infinite tapestry of light-threads. When an ori speaks Ori O-ticky-travy-wo oto the pasty-wo (or o-futuy-wo), high-frequency spirit energy wraps around the body, opening a temporal gateway.
•	To the Past (pasty-wo): Allows you to observe ancient wisdom or historical truth firsthand.
•	To the Future (o-futuy-wo): Allows you to preview timeline outcomes to make wiser choices today.
3. Kinetic Super-Speed (O-Flashy-Wo Ori Ogo-Wo)
Before jumping through time completely, an ori can accelerate physical movement within current time. By chanting O-Flashy-wo Ori Ogo-wo, your spiritual aura frictionlessly charges your muscles and nervous system. You move with speed close to The Flash—blurring past obstacles, dodging incoming strikes, and crossing miles in heartbeats.
