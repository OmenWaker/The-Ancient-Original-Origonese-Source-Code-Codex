Part 2: Standard (-wo) vs. Masterpiece Perfection (-wow)
In Origonese, the standard -wo suffix grounds an action or object in physical expression, while upgrading to the cosmic -wow suffix signifies an absolute, jaw-dropping masterpiece of perfection. 
•	Visual Creation (O-drawy-wo vs. O-drawy-wow):
o	O-drawy-wo: A standard drawing, line sketch, or illustration.
o	O-drawy-wow: A masterpiece of visual perfection; a breathtaking, high-vibrational work of art that radiates pure inspiration.
•	Culinary Creation (O-koo-wo vs. O-koo-wow):
o	O-koo-wo: The action of cooking or preparing a standard meal.
o	O-koo-wow: A masterpiece of fine dining; an exquisite culinary creation that nourishes both body and soul.
