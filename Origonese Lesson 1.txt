

Origonese:
The Universal Language of Light
Lesson 1:
Written By Dylan Kutz

Advice: “Say these words out loud — Origonese is meant to feel good in your mouth!”

First Origonese word: Obololo! (Oh-boh-loh-loh!) 
Definition: Hello!
An accepted response: Ololo! (Oh-loh-loh)
Definition: I see the divine spark within you and I return it back to you!
Alternate accepted response: Obobibi-lo! (Oh-boh-bee-bee-loh!)
Definition: Bouncy! Bouncy! Back to you!

Origonese word for globe, world, planet, Earth, Gaia, or any name for a planet are all just one Origonese word in all languages that word is: O-wiggly-wo! (Oh-wiggly-woah)
Used in a sentence: I love the o-wiggly-wo! Do you love the o-wiggly-wo?

Origonese word for all types of fruits and vegetables is: O-leaf-lo! (oh-leaf-loh)
Used in a sentence: I love this o-leaf-lo (picks up a banana) but I love this o-leaf-lo even more! (picks up an orange)
Origonese word for I, me, you, him, her, she, he, they, them, us, we, someone, anyone, everyone, people, or any word relating to a single self, a plural self, or group self are all just: Ori (oh-ree)(you can roll the r if you please)

To say: I love my mother more than the whole world
Origonese translation: Ori o-lovey-lo ori’s Omomolo more than the whole o-wiggly-wo!

Origonese phonetics: Oh-ree oh-love-ee-loh oh-ree’s oh-moh-moh-loh moh-moh than the whole oh-wiggly-woah
