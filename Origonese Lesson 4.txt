New Words for Lesson 4:
1. Origonese word for Solar System:
O-wiggly-wow (Oh-wiggly-wow)
•	Origonese Definition: A solar system, planetary system, or family of worlds dancing around a star. (Notice how one world is an o-wiggly-wo, but a whole family of worlds becomes an o-wiggly-wow!)
•	Used in a sentence: Ori loves exploring all the beautiful planets in our o-wiggly-wow!
2. Origonese word for Galaxy:
O-giggly-wow (Oh-giggly-wow)
•	Origonese Definition: A galaxy, deep space spiral, or cosmic swirl of billions of stars and worlds giggling together in harmony.
•	Used in a sentence: Our o-wiggly-wo is just one tiny bright light inside a giant o-giggly-wow!
3. Origonese word for Sun or Sunshine:
O-shiny-wo (Oh-shine-ee-woah)
•	Origonese Definition: The sun, sunshine, sunlight, or a bright star that gives light and warmth.
•	Used in a sentence: The o-shiny-wo is so warm and bright today!
4. Origonese word for No / Negativity / Low Frequency:
Zap! (Zap!) —or— Zap-no! (Zap-noh!)
•	Origonese Definition: No, stop, or resetting low frequency into light. Spoken quickly to clear away bad vibes!
•	Used in a sentence:
o	Question: Does ori want low frequency or sadness today?
o	Response: Zap! Ori only wants high frequency and joy!
